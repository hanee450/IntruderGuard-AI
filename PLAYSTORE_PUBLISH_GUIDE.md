# 📲 How to Publish IntruderGuard AI App to Google Play Store

Is guide ke zariye aap apne app ko **Google Play Store** par upload kar sakte hain taaki poori duniya me koi bhi ise direct Android phone me search aur install kar sake!

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Android `.apk` & `.aab` File Generate Karein

1. Folder me se **`BUILD_ANDROID_APK.bat`** par double click karein.
2. Yeh aapki Web App `https://hanee450.github.io/IntruderGuard-AI/` ko Android Native App Package me convert kar dega.
3. Build complete hone par aapko 2 files milengi:
   - 📱 **`app-release-signed.apk`** (Direct phone me install karne ke liye)
   - 📦 **`app-release-bundle.aab`** (Google Play Store par upload karne ke liye)

---

## 🚀 Step 2: Google Play Console Account Banayein

1. **[play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console)** par jaaein.
2. Apne Google Account se sign in karke **Google Play Developer Account** register karein (Google iske liye one-time $25 fee leta hai).

---

## 📤 Step 3: App Upload Karein Play Store Par

1. Google Play Console me **"Create App"** button par click karein.
2. Details bharein:
   - **App Name**: `IntruderGuard AI Security`
   - **Default Language**: `English / Hindi`
   - **App Category**: `Tools / Security`
3. Left menu me **Production** -> **Create new release** par click karein.
4. Step 1 me generate hui **`app-release-bundle.aab`** file upload karein.
5. App Screenshots, Privacy Policy, aur Short Description daalein.
6. **"Save"** -> **"Review release"** -> **"Start rollout to Production"** click karein!

---

🎉 **Congratulations!** 24 se 48 ghante ke andar Google aapke app ko verify karke **Play Store** par LIVE kar dega, aur koi bhi ise Play Store par search karke apne phone me download kar sakega!
